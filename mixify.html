Mixify

A modern music experience that brings listening, lyrics and friends together.

Maintained and extended by Priyanshu Sharma.

AndroidKotlinJetpack ComposeOpen source · GPL-3.0
01 / Overview

Music, with more ways to connect.

Mixify is an open-source Android music application combining music playback, discovery, synced lyrics, offline listening and social listening in a modern interface. It gives listeners a place to explore songs, organize favourites, manage playlists and enjoy music with friends.

My work focuses on maintaining the app and extending its experience with lock-screen playback, sharing features, room links and interface improvements.

02 / Features

Built around the way you listen.

01 — PLAYBACK

Music that stays with you

Music playback with background listening, a manageable queue, player controls and quick access to favourite tracks.

02 — LYRICS

Follow every line

Synced lyrics and lyric display styles make it easier to follow the song directly from the player.

03 — OFFLINE

Listen beyond your connection

Download supported music and return to it offline, with convenient access to saved content when connectivity is limited.

04 — LISTEN TOGETHER

Turn a song into a shared moment

Create or join a listening room with friends. Share the room code or invitation link to bring others into the same listening session.

05 — LOCK SCREEN

Your music at a glance

A custom lock-screen player brings playback controls, artwork and synced lyrics into the lock-screen experience.

06 — SHARE CARDS

Share the song. Share the feeling.

Create song cards or select lyric lines for a lyrics card, then share them with track information and a Mixify link.

07 — LIBRARY & DISCOVERY

Find it. Save it. Come back to it.

Search songs, artists, albums and playlists. Organize your library with favourites, playlists and recently played music.

08 — INTERFACE

A clear, modern player

A Jetpack Compose interface with appearance options, smooth navigation and accessible playback controls for everyday listening.

03 / My contributions

Extending the experience.

I maintain and customize Mixify, building on its open-source foundations. My enhancements include:

  • Custom lock screen: playback controls, lyrics presentation, background styling and layout refinements.
  • Song and lyrics cards: share previews, lyric-line selection and share layouts.
  • Room invitations: copy/share options and links for Listen Together rooms.
  • App and website links: song-sharing entry points and download access for new listeners.
  • Polish and maintenance: interface refinements, notification behaviour and playback-related fixes.

Mixify builds on existing open-source work. Original project credits, modification history and licence details are maintained in the repository documentation.

05 / Get started

Take Mixify for a spin.

  1. Open the official Mixify website using the link below.
  2. Choose the latest APK download, or find the release on GitHub.
  3. Open the APK on your Android device and allow installation from that source if prompted.
  4. Install Mixify and start exploring your music.

Developers can clone the repository, open it in Android Studio and follow the repository's build instructions.
