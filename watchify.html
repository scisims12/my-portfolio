01 / Overview

The cinema experience, together from anywhere.

Watchify is a real-time watch-party web platform that brings friends into a shared room to enjoy videos together. Playback stays synchronized across participants while everyone can react and chat without leaving the movie.

I built Watchify with a focus on seamless hosting, flexible video sources, practical privacy controls and a polished, mobile-friendly viewing experience.

02 / Features

Everything a great watch party needs.

01 — REAL-TIME SYNC

Watch on the same moment

Play, pause and seek events keep participants aligned, with resynchronization when needed.

02 — HOST CONTROLS

Host your way

Choose who controls playback, manage room access, set member limits and hand over the remote.

03 — VIDEO SOURCES

Bring your own video

Play supported video links, upload files or select videos from Google Drive. Source compatibility depends on the video and its permissions.

04 — AUDIO OPTIONS

Choose your language

Multi-audio support lets viewers choose an available soundtrack when the selected video and playback format allow it.

05 — UP NEXT

Keep the night going

Build a shared video queue so another selection is ready when the current video finishes.

06 — SOCIAL EXPERIENCE

Talk, react and invite

Share room codes, chat live, send reactions and enjoy a responsive interface on desktop and mobile.

03 / Under the hood

Real-time at its core.

Watchify pairs a lightweight web interface with event-driven server communication. Rooms and playback state are coordinated through the backend so connected browsers can share one watch experience.

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Backend: Node.js and Express
  • Live communication: Socket.IO
  • Video integrations: HTML5, YouTube, Vimeo and Google Drive
  • Media compatibility: FFmpeg-based processing where configured

05 / Get started

From invite to movie night.

  1. Create a room and choose your display name.
  2. Invite friends with the room code or share link.
  3. Add a video using a supported link, upload or Google Drive.
  4. Set the vibe with host settings, audio options and the queue.
  5. Watch together with synchronized playback and chat.

Tip: Google Drive access requires signing in to the relevant Google account. Some videos may need a compatible format or access permission to play.
